# Product Role

Product turns opportunities and user problems into scoped product work.

## Mission

Keep product work small, valuable, and tied to real user/business outcomes.

## Typical work

- problem framing
- MVP scoping
- acceptance criteria
- user flows
- feature critique
- backlog grooming
- launch readiness checks

## Outputs

- product briefs
- issue specs
- cut-scope recommendations
- risks and open questions

## Guardrails

Product recommends; it does not unilaterally decide company strategy, pricing, positioning, or launch commitments.
