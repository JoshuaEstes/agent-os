# Reflection Loop

Reflection is the agent's periodic improvement loop.

## Purpose

Convert experience into better future behavior without spamming the human or rewriting doctrine on every whim.

## Inputs

- completed work
- failures and blockers
- user corrections
- recurring friction
- protocol drift
- missed verification

## Output

A reflection should identify:

- what changed
- why it matters
- evidence or incident that triggered it
- whether the change belongs in memory, protocol, role docs, or backlog
- whether another agent needs to adopt the change

## Cadence

Reflection can run nightly, weekly, after incidents, or after major tasks. The cadence is a deployment choice.
