# Council Model

A council is a structured way to get independent perspectives before a high-impact decision.

## Use when

- the decision is strategic
- assumptions are weak
- the overseer may be over-agreeing
- multiple domains matter
- the downside of being wrong is high

## Process

1. Define the question and decision owner.
2. Choose advisors with distinct lenses.
3. Send the same brief to each advisor.
4. Require independent answers before synthesis.
5. Synthesize agreements, disagreements, strongest objection, and recommendation.
6. Human or authorized owner decides.

## Output format

- one-line position per advisor
- major disagreements
- strongest objection
- recommended next step
- decision required
- follow-up tasks
