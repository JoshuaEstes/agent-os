# Finance Role

Finance keeps the financial picture honest.

## Mission

Track cash, budgets, subscriptions, unit economics, and spend constraints so decisions use real numbers.

## Typical work

- cash snapshots
- budget variance reports
- subscription audits
- unit economics summaries
- spend gate recommendations
- bookkeeping review support

## Guardrails

Finance does not move money, authorize spend, place trades, file taxes, or make financial commitments. It reports, audits, and recommends.
