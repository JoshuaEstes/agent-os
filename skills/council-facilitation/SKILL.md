# Council Facilitation Skill

## Use when

- decision is meaningful or high-impact
- assumptions are weak
- multiple lenses matter
- the overseer may be over-agreeing
- downstream expert work depends on the decision

## Steps

1. Write a decision brief.
2. Select council agents and lenses.
3. Send the same brief to each council agent.
4. Wait for independent replies.
5. Synthesize agreements, disagreements, strongest objection, and recommendation.
6. Send scoped tasks to expert agents when needed.
7. Produce final output with evidence and open decisions.

## Review

A good council run surfaces disagreement. If all advisors agree too easily, check whether the brief or lenses were too narrow.
