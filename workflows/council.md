# Council Workflow

A council is a structured multi-perspective review before a significant decision.

## Inputs

- decision question
- why now
- known constraints
- decision owner
- deadline
- advisor lenses
- expected output format

## Advisor response format

Each advisor should return:

- position
- reasoning
- strongest objection
- hidden assumption
- recommended next action
- confidence level

## Synthesis format

The overseer synthesis should include:

- one-line position per advisor
- areas of agreement
- explicit disagreements
- strongest objection
- options considered
- recommendation
- decision required
- follow-up tasks

## Safety

Council output is advisory. It does not override human permission gates or deployment policy.
